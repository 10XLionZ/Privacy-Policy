Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to Pokowin ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate as a Walk & Earn application under the package name com.poko.pokowin. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By installing and using Pokowin, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Permissions

To provide our core service of tracking steps and rewarding user activity without required monetary investments, our application requests access to specific sensitive device features:

Required Sensitive Permissions:

  • Physical Activity / Activity Recognition (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): This permission is strictly required to detect and count your daily steps. We use this data solely to calculate your step rewards and coin generation inside the app.
  • Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): We access location data to accurately measure distance traveled, validate authentic movement patterns, and prevent fraudulent activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information collected through device permissions is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To track your step count and convert physical steps into in-app virtual coins.
  • To maintain and improve the core performance, stability, and UI of the application.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity (such as simulated walking or step-tampering).

3. Data Protection and Retention

We implement robust security measures to guarantee your data is protected. Your physical activity and location data are processed directly on your device or transmitted securely using encrypted protocols (HTTPS/SSL). We do not rent, sell, or share your personal activity logs or location history with third-party advertising networks or external data brokers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates become effective immediately upon being posted on this webpage.
